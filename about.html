Our Data & Methodology

Every number on NyayClock traces to a public government source. Here's exactly what we use and where we can be wrong.

Data Freshness Policy

We never show an unlabeled number. Every statistic carries one of three badges:

● LIVE — fetched directly from NJDG's public dashboard at page load. If the government site is reachable, pending counts are real-time.
● SNAPSHOT · N days old — NJDG was unreachable (government sites have downtime); we serve our last verified crawl and state its age.
● OFFICIAL SNAPSHOT · date — datasets like state-wise pendency are published periodically by data.gov.in; we show the official reference date, never pretend they're live.

Monthly instituted/disposed figures come from the NJDG dashboard's own monthly counters and update as NJDG updates them.

How The Predictor Works

For each case type we model three numbers: median disposal time, the 25th percentile ("fastest quarter") and 75th percentile ("slowest quarter"). These are anchored on NJDG case-age distributions — e.g., if 41% of pending cheque-bounce cases are over 3 years old, the median cannot plausibly be under ~3 years — cross-checked against DoJ disposal studies and Daksh's litigant surveys. Forum multipliers (commercial courts ≈ 40% faster; High Courts ≈ 35% slower) follow published comparative studies.

Honest Limitations

① Past ≠ future. We assume historical disposal patterns continue. Infrastructure changes (new courts, fast-track schemes) can shift them.
② Medians hide variance. Two identical-looking cases can take wildly different paths based on evidence volume, witness availability and judge workload. That's why we show ranges.
③ Data lag. NJDG updates near-real-time but data.gov.in tables are snapshots (Dec 2022/2023). We label every table with its reference date.
④ No case-level prediction. We cannot see your specific case file. Complexity, cooperation of parties and local court conditions all matter beyond any statistical model.
⑤ State variation is real. A "median" nationally can be double or half in your specific district. Our district-level refinement is on the roadmap as NJDG district feeds are parsed.
