Free Legal Aid & Government Money You May Be Owed

Most Indians who qualify for a free government lawyer don't know it. And crores in victim compensation goes unclaimed every year. Check what applies to you — takes 2 minutes.

🆓 Am I Eligible For A Free Government Lawyer?

Under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. Answer honestly — this is informational, the DLSA makes the final call.

💰 Victim Compensation — Money The Government Owes Victims

Paid even if the accused is never caught (Supreme Court, Nipun Saxena v. UOI). Applied through the DLSA — the same office that gives free lawyers.

🏛️ Support Schemes That Help During Disputes

Schemes that provide money, shelter, insurance or healthcare when you're fighting a case.

How to actually apply for free legal aid: Walk into your District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) — it's in every district court complex — OR apply online at NALSA's LSAMS portal (no login needed, available in 10 languages), OR just call 15100. Bring photo ID and a brief written summary of your problem. Women, children, SC/ST, disabled persons and prisoners need NO income proof.
