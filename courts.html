📈 Clearance Rate Visualized (worst first)

Clearance rate = cases disposed ÷ cases filed. Above 100% means the backlog is shrinking. Below means it's growing.

Why this matters: Two identical cases can take twice as long in a district with 3,000 cases/judge vs one with 1,500. Filing strategy (which jurisdiction, whether to seek transfer, whether to settle early) should account for court conditions.
