What kind of cases are pending?

Criminal cases outnumber civil ~3.5:1 — most Indians fighting the system are in criminal proceedings.

How old are the pending cases?

— A case filed today competes with lakhs of decade-old ones for the same judge hours.

The human cost — undertrials in prison

73.5% of India's 5.30 lakh prisoners are undertrials — legally innocent, awaiting trial. Source: NCRB Prison Statistics 2023.

Worst states by district-court pendency

Source: data.gov.in (Dec 2023 snapshot). UP alone holds more pending cases than many countries' entire judiciary.

What a dispute actually costs

DAKSH access-to-justice research: average dispute takes 1,446 days (~4 years) and costs 31% of claim value in legal + court fees — among the world's highest. This is why forum choice and settlement strategy matter more than most people realize.
