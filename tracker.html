Track Your Case Like a Pro

Every case filed in India's eCourts system gets a 16-character CNR number. With it (or even without it), you can track everything yourself — for free.

What Is a CNR Number?

A Case Number Record (CNR) is a unique 16-digit alphanumeric ID (e.g., DLCT010012342019) assigned to every case. It appears on your case filing receipt, summons, and every court order. If you've lost it, you can search by party name, advocate name, FIR number, or case type + year + court.

How To Track Your Case — Step by Step

Go to the official eCourts portal

Visit services.ecourts.gov.in — this is the government's free official service. Beware of paid lookalike sites.

Choose "Case Status"

Enter your CNR number, or use "Case Number" / "Party Name" search if you don't have it. Party-name search needs your state, district and court complex.

Read the case status screen

You'll see: filing date, registration date, first hearing, next hearing date, stage of case, judge name, and every order passed. Use our status decoder below.

Download orders & judgments

Every order PDF is free to download from the same screen. Keep copies — they're needed for appeals and execution.

Set up your own reminders

eCourts offers SMS/email updates via its "Case Status" app (Play Store/App Store, official eCommittee SC app). Enable notifications so you never miss a hearing.

Watch for excessive gaps

If no hearing is listed for 6+ months, or dates are deferred repeatedly without progress, that's when you escalate — see Know Your Rights.
